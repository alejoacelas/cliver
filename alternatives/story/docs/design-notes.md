# Storyboard concept notes

Reference: [Rough Draft](https://www.roughdraft.md/) and its [homepage storyboard plan](https://github.com/Lex-Inc/roughdraft/blob/main/docs/plans/2026-05-07-homepage-workflow-storyboard.md).

## What the reference does

- States one job in a large, high-contrast hero.
- Highlights the two actions that distinguish the product.
- Shows the product before explaining it.
- Turns an abstract workflow into numbered scenes.
- Uses real in-page mock interfaces rather than screenshot-only explanation.
- Repeats one concrete example across the chat, work surface, and outcome.
- Keeps the top-level palette restrained so status colors carry meaning.

## Cliver story

The repeated example is one synthetic DNA order from a university researcher:

1. A sequence screen routes the order to customer review.
2. The reviewer starts with name, institution, email, ORCID, and intended work.
3. Cliver checks affiliation, institution type, and email-domain ownership.
4. Cliver searches the U.S. Consolidated Screening List.
5. Cliver finds customer-authored work relevant to the order.
6. A human opens the citations, requests clarification if needed, and decides what happens next.

The page stops short of implying that Cliver approves or rejects orders. The tool gathers and structures evidence; the reviewer owns follow-up and fulfillment.
