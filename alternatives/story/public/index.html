One order, six scenes

What customer screening looks like.

A sequence match is not a verdict. It starts a review of who the customer is and whether their work fits the order.

  1. 01

    An order needs review

    Sequence screening routes a potentially sensitive order to a customer reviewer.

  2. 02

    Start with the claim

    Name, institution, email, ORCID, and intended work become the research brief.

  3. 03

    Verify identity

    Cliver checks affiliation, institution type, and whether the email domain belongs to it.

  4. 04

    Search restrictions

    The customer and institution are checked against U.S. sanctions and export-control lists.

  5. 05

    Find relevant work

    Publications and research records show whether prior laboratory work fits the order.

  6. 06

    A human decides

    The reviewer opens the citations, follows up where needed, and retains the fulfillment decision.

The boundary matters

Cliver gathers.
People decide.

The model does the repetitive public-record search: affiliation, institution, domain, sanctions, and relevant work.

The reviewer checks the sources, handles ambiguity, contacts the customer, and owns the order decision.
